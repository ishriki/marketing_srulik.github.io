02 / Audience research

Two audiences. Different needs.

Two illustrative personas shape the messaging: a home cook looking for flavor and convenience, and a chef focused on quality and reliable sourcing.

David · Home cook

Seeks fresh flavors, cooking inspiration, clear pricing, and easy reordering.

Elena · Professional chef

Values traceability, consistent quality, and efficient sourcing.

What this means for marketing
  • For home cooks: share simple recipes, cooking tips, and easy ways to reorder.
  • For chefs: provide clear sourcing details, ingredient quality information, and practical product specifications.

04 / Customer journey

Guiding discovery toward loyalty.

Five stages connect discovery with trust, purchase, and loyalty. Expand each stage to explore proposed actions and metrics.

AwarenessExplore actions +

Help new people discover the brand

  • Post short Instagram and TikTok videos comparing fresh and older spices.
  • Show spices blooming in a pan to highlight aroma and color.
  • Share behind-the-scenes farm and sourcing stories.
  • Track reach, video views, and visits to the website.
InterestExplore actions +

Help visitors learn more

  • Publish easy recipes and one-minute spice technique videos.
  • Explain freshness, origins, and how different spices taste.
  • Use Instagram Stories with questions and links to helpful guides.
  • Track content saves, clicks, and time spent on articles.
ConsiderationExplore actions +

Give shoppers a reason to trust the brand

  • Offer a first-order 20% discount in exchange for email sign-up.
  • Share chef tips, product details, sourcing information, and customer reviews.
  • Make pricing, shipping, and product sizes easy to understand.
  • Track email sign-ups and product-page visits.
ConversionExplore actions +

Turn interest into a purchase

  • Show real customer feedback and cooking demonstrations.
  • Highlight a spice starter kit and a clear Shop or Claim Offer button.
  • Send a reminder to shoppers who leave items in their cart.
  • Track add-to-cart actions, completed orders, and conversion rate.
LoyaltyExplore actions +

Encourage repeat purchases and sharing

  • Send useful recipes and cooking guides to existing customers.
  • Offer timely restock reminders and occasional member benefits.
  • Invite customers to share photos of meals and favorite spice combinations.
  • Track repeat orders, email engagement, and referrals.

05 / Experiment design

Testing creative choices.

Three proposed A/B tests explore how headlines, imagery, and calls to action could influence response. Results have not been measured.

Headline A vs. B

A: “Cook like a chef at home.” B: “Explore our fresh spice collection.” Compare which message earns more clicks.

Image A vs. B

A: Spices arranged in a studio photo. B: Spices added to food during cooking. Compare which image attracts more clicks.

Button A vs. B

A: A terracotta button promoting an offer. B: A deep-teal button inviting visitors to browse products. Compare click-through rates.
